# Hot honey crispy chicken

Link: https://www.instagram.com/p/DSPr1-0AnH8/

- Marinate chicken in: 300ml milk, pepper, salt, paprika.
- Double coat in: 2 tbsp maicena, 5 tbsp flour, 1 tsp baking powder, salt, pepper, paprika
- Fry
- Cook in honey sauce: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 4 tbsp chili sauce, 3 tbsp honey, 50ml water
